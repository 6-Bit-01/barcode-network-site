SHARED STAGE PICKUPS / THREE USES

Claim it. Use it. Hurl it.

Pickups arrive on the stage during a round. Move close and press Throw to crouch, reach down, and lift one. The weapon enters your hand at contact; a hit can interrupt the pickup. Standing high punch uses it; your crouching and jumping attacks stay available. A nearby opponent takes priority on Throw. Hold Down + Throw to deliberately hurl your weapon, or press Throw outside grab range.

Solid thrown weapons lodge in an unguarded opponent and stay through their movement and falls. Face and mask damage, blood-soaked clothes, ragged tears and leg scrapes build with landed hits. Energy shots leave charred burns. Blood collects on the floor and battered falls leave smears. Battle wear lasts until you restart; blocked attacks leave no wounds. In Inspect moves, compare Fresh, Bloodied, Battered and Ravaged on any pose, or inspect damage from your current fight.

Neural SpikeA powered stabbing weapon. Close contact, three charged strikes; throw the spike to lodge it in your opponent.

Pulse DriverA compact energy weapon. Three ranged shots; throw the solid driver when you want to embed it.

Choose Weapon practice to try a nearby pickup against a stationary opponent. Player two uses ↓ + 6 to hurl in Two players.

Hold Down + high punch for an uppercut, low punch for a crouching jab, high kick for a rising knee or boot, and low kick for a sweep. Each is a separate attack. During a jump, either punch throws an air punch and either kick throws an air kick. Land one attack per jump. Sweeps beat standing guard; jumping attacks beat crouching guard. While standing, press both punches together for Double punch, or both kicks together for Power kick. Connect J → U → I for a kick finisher or U → J → U for a double-punch finisher. Keep the sequence short: no more than 0.6s between buttons. The first two hits must land; blocks, misses and interruptions break the sequence. Finishers have recovery, so commit carefully.
