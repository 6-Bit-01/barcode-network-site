# Pursuit rig and destruction art

These are new painted assets for the Cache Road overhaul, made with the built-in
`image_gen` tool on October 1, 2026. The anonymous enforcement rig introduces no
new named lore antagonist. Its rear-facing design matches the established dark
mechanical vehicle art, mint hardware, coral lights and illustrated black ink.

The two originals in `sources/` retain the exact generated bytes and transparent
RGBA. The runtime WebP files use exact lossless encoding, including invisible RGB;
decoded RGBA was compared byte for byte against each prepared atlas. Prompts,
source/runtime hashes, alpha coverage, extraction rectangles and anchors are in
the adjacent prompt files and `atlas-metadata.json`.

## Extraction and review

The initial 1536 × 1024 rig sheet painted the open ram wing slightly across the
nominal boundary between frames 2 and 3. A targeted imagegen spacing review was
inspected, but it changed the lower row's wheel heights and was not selected.
Its exact review prompt is retained; the review output is not consumed.

The original art was extracted and repacked without painting, recoloring,
background removal or resampling. The two adjacent top-row extraction rectangles
split at source x = 1138, retaining the complete ram wing in its own pose and
omitting its small fragment from the preceding pose. All eight poses use 512²
runtime cells with transparent horizontal padding. The lower row is placed one
pixel lower to align its tire contact with the upper row. Only transparent bottom
padding was cropped. Exact source rectangles and insertion offsets are recorded
in the metadata. The impact sheet is converted directly without repacking.

## Runtime ownership

`BARCODE.CacheRoadBossArt.drawRig(ctx, {x, y, width, height, health, phase,
elapsedMs, reduced, alpha})` consumes the shared `PresentationAssets` cache.
`x` is centered and `y` is tire contact; width/height describe the whole 512²
cell, including transparent padding. Health 3 → 2 → 1 → 0 selects intact,
scanner-broken, core-exposed and defeated poses. Explicit phases select scanning,
ram, armor-break or core-hit variants. Reduced Motion retains static damage
states. The accepted elapsed time is owned by the caller; this helper creates no
clock, callback, canvas, loader, input handler or physical state.

`drawImpact(ctx, {x, y, width, height, kind, progress, alpha, angle, reduced})`
draws one requested effect at the caller's projected event location. Progress is
0–1 and supplied by the road. Reduced Motion omits these moving effects.

Actual attack warning lanes, collision geometry, boss health, victory and
front/rear positions remain the road/controller's responsibility. These sprites
contain no false lane target or large full-screen flash. Hosted delivery is pinned
to the immutable art commit during publication, with bundled fallback retained.
