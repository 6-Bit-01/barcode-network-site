# Confirmed bar paint

Created with the built-in image generator from the existing beat phrase and
pulse pad as style references. The first draft looked too much like a pickup.
The final edit prompt removed its arrow and enclosed badge:

> A continuous, top-down lane surface marking with two chipped longitudinal
> cream edges, a translucent jade/cyan wash, four short diagonal beat slashes,
> and small magenta/amber ink scuffs. Open ends let successive bars form a
> four-bar stretch. Transparent background; no icon, arrow, text, asphalt,
> floating UI, or surrounding glow.

`confirmed-bar-source.png` is the transparent 512×256 RGBA production source;
`../confirmed-bar.webp` is the lossless runtime version.
